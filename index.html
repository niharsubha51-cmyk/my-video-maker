🚀 Creator Checklist

Your free production workflow

  1. Generate and edit your original story and characters.
  2. Copy scene prompts into your preferred AI video tool to create cinematic clips.
  3. Use the free WebM storyboard as a preview or reference.
  4. Assemble real footage in an editor and add licensed audio.
  5. Upload manually in YouTube Studio and mark kids content correctly.
🔒 Nothing is automatically uploaded to YouTube. This MVP makes no external requests and uses local storage for saved projects.
